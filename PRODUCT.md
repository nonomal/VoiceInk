# Product

## Register

product

## Users

macOS users who dictate frequently and want fast, private text entry without leaving their current application. Their primary workflow is recording speech, reviewing the result when necessary, and inserting it at the cursor with minimal interruption.

## Product Purpose

VoiceInk is a native macOS voice-to-text utility focused on fast transcription, local processing, context-aware enhancement, and dependable delivery into the application the user is already working in. Success means accurate text reaches the cursor quickly while history, models, modes, and privacy controls remain easy to inspect and manage.

## Brand Personality

Efficient, private, and quietly joyful. The product should feel native to macOS, direct in its language, and confident without becoming visually loud.

## Anti-references

Avoid browser-like dashboard chrome, decorative AI imagery, oversized controls, and interaction patterns that interrupt the current application when a compact utility surface is sufficient.

## Design Principles

- Keep users in their current application whenever possible.
- Make the next action obvious and keyboard-friendly.
- Show the provenance and state of generated text clearly.
- Use familiar macOS behaviors and visual conventions.
- Keep advanced management available without adding friction to frequent workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native macOS controls, keyboard navigation, VoiceOver labels, sufficient text contrast, and reduced-motion-compatible transitions. Do not rely on color alone to communicate transcription or enhancement state.
